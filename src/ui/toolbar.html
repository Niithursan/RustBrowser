Browsing preferences

Disabling restoration removes saved session URLs and session recovery files. It does not close active tabs. Private windows never retain either history or sessions.

Clear browsing data

Clearing closes every tab in this window and leaves a blank page. Finish or cancel downloads first. Cookies and site storage are shared by this window's tabs; clearing can sign you out. Bookmarks and downloaded files remain. Library recovery backups are retained to protect recovered bookmarks and may contain old history or permissions. This is not secure disk erasure. New browsing can create new data.
